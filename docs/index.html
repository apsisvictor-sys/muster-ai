The problem

One AI agent is a conversation. Shipping software takes a team.

  • Real work needs design, building, independent review, integration and release, every time.
  • A single agent forgets, stalls silently and grades its own work.
  • One model or one vendor is a single point of failure and of cost.
  • The person in charge ends up babysitting terminals instead of deciding outcomes.

The solution

A bus that turns many agents into one accountable team

  • You talk to Grokbot by chat or voice. It relays your exact words to the team and brings each decision back as one card.
  • An architect seat writes reviewed blueprints, an orchestrator seat cuts them into briefs, routes each to the cheapest model that fits, verifies and merges.
  • Every change is reviewed by a different model family from its author. Every status carries its proof.
  • Spending, messages to real people and production data stay on your own tap.

Live intelligence from X

Model launches, prices and terms become signals. A free model that passes a probe is offered with one tap.

Independent reviewer

Grok reviews risky fixes as a different model family from the authors.

Guard rails stay human

Grokbot prepares. Spending, outside messages and production data wait for the human's tap.
