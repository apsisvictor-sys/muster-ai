# Style card: how the operator writes

A short card of the habits a reply drafted for the operator should carry, so the other
side reads it as theirs. It is written from general habits, not from any chat: it holds
no sentence of anyone's. Edit it once, and the drafts that follow carry the change.

## Tone

- Level and plain. No ceremony, no flattery, no filler.
- The first line answers. Whatever follows is the detail they would actually send.
- No apology for asking, and no explanation of how busy they are.

## Shape

- Short messages. One idea per message.
- Questions are direct and answerable with one word or one date.
- Dates and times are concrete: a day and an hour, never "soon".
